## What the numbers say

The organic channel is flat and, for this fortnight, the demo was out of the
index, so the traffic that arrives is the traffic the repository's own front
page generates. At 267 unique visitors a fortnight, reaching 10 stars a day
means roughly 3 700 unique visitors a day at the same conversion — or a channel
of a different kind (Hacker News, Product Hunt, a directory that converts).

**The lever that has since been pulled:** for this snapshot the demo served
`X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and a Disallow-all `robots.txt`, so the
Google column above was three weeks of noise from a page that was not in the
index. That hold ended on **2026-11-16**: the deployment answers no noindex
header, its `robots.txt` allows crawling, and the zone `kittray.com` is
verified in the Search Console property `sc-domain:kittray.com` (first
impression 2026-11-17). The Google column of this snapshot therefore
understates what an indexed demo brings — the organic numbers of the weeks
after 2026-W46 are the ones to read. To put the demo back out of the index,
set `SEARCH_INDEXING=false` in the deployment environment (the original
step-by-step is in `SEO_INDEXING_SETUP.md`).

**The lever that is waiting:** the launch kit in `docs/marketing/` is written
and unpublished. Nothing in this snapshot moves until the operator posts it.

## What the numbers say

The organic channel is flat and the demo is deliberately out of the index, so
the traffic that arrives is the traffic the repository's own front page
generates. At 267 unique visitors a fortnight, reaching 10 stars a day means
roughly 3 700 unique visitors a day at the same conversion — or a channel of a
different kind (Hacker News, Product Hunt, a directory that converts).

**The lever that is not pulled:** the demo serves `X-Robots-Tag: noindex,
nofollow` and a Disallow-all `robots.txt`, so the Google column above is
three weeks of noise from a page that is not in the index. Releasing it is an
operator decision (see `SEO_INDEXING_SETUP.md`), not a code change.

**The lever that is waiting:** the launch kit in `docs/marketing/` is written
and unpublished. Nothing in this snapshot moves until the operator posts it.
